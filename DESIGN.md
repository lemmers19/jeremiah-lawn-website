# Design system

## Direction

A lawn is part of the welcome home. The page uses a vivid field-green identity and a welcoming, editorial rhythm rather than a generic list of service cards.

## Palette

- **Spruce** `#17352a`: brand foundation, large dark sections, and primary controls.
- **Deep spruce** `#10291f`: depth and contrast.
- **Citrus** `#d7f36a`: high-visibility actions, highlights, and page transitions.
- **Canvas** `#f3f2e9`: warm, muted page ground.
- **Paper** `#fffef9`: text on dark areas.
- **Ink** `#1b2e26`: main text on light areas.

## Typography

- **Barlow Condensed** for large display headings, with a strong, compact silhouette.
- **Manrope** for body text, navigation, and controls.
- System sans-serif fallbacks keep the page readable if web fonts are unavailable.

## Composition and components

- Split, photo-led opening with a deep spruce text field and a broad lawn image.
- Use the supplied Jeremiah's Lawn Care mower wordmark in the header and footer; blend its light image background into each surface.
- Open editorial sections with generous spacing, asymmetric text columns, and custom line illustrations.
- Rounded pill actions and thin rules; no repeating card grid.
- Small-screen navigation collapses into an accessible menu button; content stacks into a single column.
- Use the business's supplied photos and confirmed service and contact details.

## Motion and accessibility

- The opening image settles into place once; reduced-motion preferences disable the animation.
- Semantic landmarks, skip link, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image text, and responsive layouts are included.
